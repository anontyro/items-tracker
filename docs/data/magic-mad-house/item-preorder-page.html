Introducing two NEW playable villains to Ravensburger’s award-winning Villainous strategy game system! Retell your favorite Disney stories from the villain’s point of view in this expandalone box. Will everything go according to plan this time? Only if you play your cards right! This expandalone adds ALL-NEW gameplay and can be played on its own or combined with other Disney Villainous games. Every Villainous expansion adds something new to master! Each Villain’s deck of cards is designed with a unique gameplay strategy inspired by the films you love; no two villains work toward the same goal, and these two villains are no exception.

The heart of gameplay is simple. Move your Villain’s sculpted mover, use actions to play your cards, and draw from your opponents’ Fate deck to disrupt their carefully made plans with meddling heroes. But as in all Villainous games, each’s villain’s objective is unique.
